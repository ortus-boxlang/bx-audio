## [Unreleased]

### Added

* The BxAudio theme: example `15-theme`, a grid anthem at 132 BPM with an original adventure fanfare (`bx-audio render theme`, and `--sting` for a 3 second jingle). BxAudio logos and brand colors (teal to blue) across the docs, the explorer and the readme
* A custom landing page for the docs (`docs/.theme/home.bxm`) with press-to-play examples, a browser playground and the ecosystem, and a "Try it" page with a run-tested recipe for every feature and a clone-and-run guide
* Examples `13-trailer` (music fitted to a video, a voice-over with captions, ducking, mastering, chapters and mux) and `14-game-audio` (a mixer session with buses, loops, variations, a boss theme from stretch and pitch, and a sprite)
* Game audio: `audioMixer` (real time, buses, volume, pan, pitch, loops, crossfade; output to the speakers, a clock with no sound, or memory), `audioPlay`, `audioLoopify`, `audioVariations`, `audioSprite` (with a Howler.js map). CLI `play`, `loopify`, `sprite`
* Speech for video: `audioSpeak` takes `speed` and `pauses`; `audioSpeakCues` returns the audio with timed caption lines; `audioSubtitles` writes SRT and WebVTT. CLI `speak --speed --captions --by-clause`
* FFmpeg as an optional separate program: `audioFfmpeg`, `audioFfmpegInstall` (pinned, SHA-256 checked downloads for Linux and macOS on amd64 and arm64, and Windows), OGG, Opus and AAC output through `audioSave`, reading any format and the audio of any video through `audioLoad`, `videoInfo`, `audioMux` and `audioMarkers`. CLI `ffmpeg`, `probe`, `mux`. Settings `ffmpeg`, `BX_AUDIO_FFMPEG`
* FLAC read and write in plain Java, verified against FFmpeg (identical samples, CRC and MD5 valid)
* Loudness and mastering: `audioLoudness` (EBU R128 / BS.1770, within 0.1 LU of FFmpeg's ebur128), `audioNormalizeLoudness`, `audioMaster` with presets, `audioCompress`, `audioLimit`, `audioEq`. CLI `loudness`, `master`
* Time and pitch: `audioStretch`, `audioFitTo`, `audioPitch` (WSOLA). CLI `stretch`, `pitch`
* Analysis: `audioBpm` (tempo and beats), `audioSpectrum`, `audioSpectrogram`, `audioFft`, `audioDetectSilence`, `audioTrimSilence`. CLI `bpm`, `silence`
* Downloads now live in `~/.bxaudio` (settings `home`, `BX_AUDIO_HOME`), shared by every BoxLang install. `audioStorage`, `audioStorageClear` and the CLI `where` and `clean` show and free them. The speech engine loads through the module's own class loader
* Voices: all 177 voices of the collection are classified. `audioVoices( status=, license= )`, `audioLicenses` and CLI `voices --excluded --downloadable --all`, `voices <voice>` and `licenses` show what is offered, what is not and why
* Example `12-voiceover`: a spoken announcement over music that ducks under the voice, using `audioSpeak` and `audioDuck`, or a recording of your own. Command line only, since it needs the speech engine
* Examples `10-jazz` (a twelve bar blues with a walking bass and a solo improvised by rules that follow the chords) and `11-rock` (distorted power chords, bass, drums and a seeded solo), measured, tested and in the explorer
* Example `09-sfx`: ten synthesized game sound effects (jump, coin, laser, explosion, power up, hit, blip, alarm, game over, footstep), as a sampler, one sound, or a folder of files
* Example `08-chiptune`: five original 8 bit tracks (overworld, cave, boss, title and victory) written as text in a small notation, for two pulse channels, a triangle bass and noise drums. In the explorer as well
* Text to speech: `audioSpeak`, `audioVoices`, `audioVoiceInstall`, `audioVoiceRemove` and `audioSpeechStatus`, and the `bx-audio` commands `speak`, `voices`, `install` and `remove`. The Voices engine (about 148 MB, pinned by SHA-1) and the voices are downloaded on first use, verified and resumable, into the `bx-audio` folder of the BoxLang home (settings `home` and `autoInstall`). The catalog lists only single speaker US English voices whose model card states an open license, generated by `tools/update-voices.bxs`, with the list and the voices left out in `docs/voices.md`
* `audioSave` writes MP3 without a `bitrate` at 192 kbps, or 128 kbps below 32 kHz where 192 does not exist
* The `bx-audio` command line: `info`, `convert`, `normalize`, `trim`, `waveform`, `pieces`, `render`, `explorer` and `doctor`. It runs as `boxlang module:bxaudio <command>`, and `install-bx-module` installs a `bx-audio` executable and bash completion from the `executable` and `completions` settings in `box.json`. The module now ships the examples and the explorer
* The example scripts now let settings preset by code (`overrides`) win over the command line
* Examples: `05-techno`, `06-synthwave` and `07-cinematic` (trailer music with a true silent beat before the boom), measured and covered by `ExamplesGenresTest`, and added to the explorer
* Catalog explorer in `examples/explorer`: a BoxLang MiniServer app (`index.bxm`, `render.bxm`) that plays every catalog piece in the browser with adjustable settings, a waveform, seeking and a render cache. BoxLang branded with the official logos and palette, light and dark. Covered by `ExplorerCatalogTest`
* `audioWaveform( audio, points )` returns per-slice peak levels for drawing a waveform
* MP3 read and write through `audioLoad` and `audioSave` (new `bitrate` argument). Uses LGPL JLayer and jump3r as separate optional jars in `libs/`, with license texts in `licenses/`; files are detected by content, not extension
* Module scaffold from the BoxLang module template
* `AudioBuffer` type and a dependency free WAV codec (read PCM 8/16/24/32 and float 32/64, write 16/24 bit PCM and 32 bit float)
* BIFs `audioSilence`, `audioLoad`, `audioSave` and `audioInfo`
* Effects: `audioDelay` (with feedback, ping pong and an automatic tail), `audioReverb` (Freeverb design) and `audioChorus`
* `audioMix` to sum buffers
* Examples: `02-lofi`, `03-house` and `04-ambient`, original compositions checked by measurement (chord tones, brightness, beat pulse, transient strength, a silent ending) and covered by tests
* Examples catalog started with `01-pop-drive`, a BoxLang port of a numpy script checked against the original (same length and peak, octave bands within 0.5 dB, kick and bass envelope correlation 1.000), covered by a test
* Build: `shadowJar` now depends on `serviceLoaderBuild`, and `build/module` is cleared before it is assembled, so a jar built on its own still registers its BIFs and no removed file lingers
* Synthesis: `audioFreq`, `audioOsc` (sine, saw, square, triangle, pulse, additive), `audioSweep`, `audioNoise` (white, pink, brown, blue), `audioEnvelope`, `audioDrum` (kick, snare, hat, openhat, clap, tom, crash, riser) and `audioFilter` (lowpass, highpass, bandpass, notch)
* Timeline mixer: `audioTimeline`, `audioAdd`, `audioBeats`, `audioTrackGain`, `audioPump`, `audioDuck` and `audioRender`, with a tempo grid, automatic sample rate and channel conversion, kick-style pump ducking and voice-over sidechain ducking
* `audioSaturate` for peak-normalize plus tanh limiting
* DSP BIFs `audioGain`, `audioPan`, `audioFade`, `audioNormalize`, `audioTrim`, `audioConcat`, `audioReverse`, `audioChannels` and `audioResample`
