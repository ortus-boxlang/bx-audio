A BoxLang module · pure JVM

Make a kick from code. Master it. Make it speak.

Synthesize instruments and drums, arrange them on a beat grid, master to a loudness target, speak text with open license voices, score a video and play sound in a game. No Python. Nothing native to install.

$install-bx-module bx-audio

groove.bxs
t = audioTimeline( bpm=128 );
kick = audioDrum( "kick" );
bass = audioFilter(
    audioEnvelope( audioOsc( "saw", audioFreq( "A1" ), 0.4 ), 0.003, 0.12 ),
    "lowpass", 900, 2 );

for( at in audioBeats( t, 0, 16 ) ){
    audioAdd( timeline=t, sound=kick, at=at, track="drums" );
}
audioPump( t, "bass", audioBeats( t, 0, 16 ), -5, 0.18 );

mix = audioMaster( audio=audioRender( t ), target="streaming" );
audioSave( mix, "groove.mp3" );  // -14 LUFS

Everything on the JVM

One module. The whole audio pipeline.

Synthesis and mixing

Band-limited oscillators, drums, filters, a tempo grid, named tracks and kick pumping.

Text to speech

Open license voices that download on first use, with speed, pauses and captions timed to the words.

Score a video

Cut music to the exact length of a picture, add a voice-over and mux it with FFmpeg. audioMux()

Loudness and mastering

Measure the EBU R128 way and deliver to a target with a mastering chain, compressor, limiter and EQ.

Streaming-14 LUFS
Podcast-16 LUFS
Broadcast-23 LUFS

Game audio

A real time mixer with buses, loops and crossfades, varied effects and audio sprites.

Time and pitch

Stretch, pitch shift and fit a clip to an exact length without the chipmunk.

Analysis

FFT, spectrum, tempo and silence detection, plus true peak.

A real command line

bx-audio info, master, speak and more. Downloads live in ~/.bxaudio.
