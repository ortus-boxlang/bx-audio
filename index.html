    BoxLang Audio

    Make sound from code. Synthesize instruments and drums, arrange them on a beat grid, mix with ducking, add effects, master to a loudness target, speak text with open license voices, score a video, and play sound in a game. It all runs on the JVM, in BoxLang, with no Python and nothing native to install.

    t = audioTimeline( bpm=128 );
kick = audioDrum( "kick" );
bass = audioFilter( audioEnvelope( audioOsc( "saw", audioFreq( "A1" ), 0.4 ), 0.003, 0.12 ), "lowpass", 900, 2 );

for( at in audioBeats( t, 0, 16 ) ){
    audioAdd( timeline=t, sound=kick, at=at, track="drums" );
}
audioAdd( timeline=t, sound=bass, at=t.bar( 1 ), track="bass", db=-6 );
audioPump( t, "bass", audioBeats( t, 0, 16 ), -5, 0.18 );

mix = audioMaster( audio=audioRender( t ), target="streaming" );   // -14 LUFS, true peak under -1 dBFS
audioSave( mix, "groove.mp3" );
    Get started

    Install the module, make a sound, and meet the bx-audio command line.

    Loudness and mastering

    Measure loudness the EBU R128 way and deliver to streaming, podcast or broadcast targets.

    Speak and subtitle

    Open license voices that download on first use, with speed, pauses and captions timed to the words.

    What is in it

    AreaWhat you get
    SynthesisBand-limited oscillators, noise, envelopes, sweeps, drums, filters
    MixingA tempo grid, named tracks, kick pumping, voice-over ducking
    EffectsDelay with ping pong, Freeverb reverb, chorus, saturation
    MasteringEBU R128 loudness, a mastering chain, compressor, limiter, EQ
    Time and pitchTime stretch, fit to an exact length, pitch shift
    SpeechText to speech with open license voices, speed, pauses, captions
    VideoRead any video, subtitles, chapter markers, put sound on a picture
    GamesReal time mixer, seamless loops, effect variations, audio sprites
    AnalysisTempo and beats, spectrum, silence
    FormatsWAV, MP3 and FLAC built in; OGG, Opus and AAC through FFmpeg

    Requirements

    BoxLang 1.x on Java 21. WAV, FLAC, analysis and everything that makes sound need nothing else. MP3 uses two small LGPL jars shipped with the module and the JDK's java.desktop module. Text to speech downloads its engine and a voice the first time, and FFmpeg is found on your computer or downloaded when something needs it. Downloads go to ~/.bxaudio and you can see and clear them with bx-audio where and bx-audio clean.
