    // The hero motif: rising fourths, then a dotted turn
motif = [ [ 0, 69, 4 ], [ 4, 74, 6 ], [ 10, 76, 2 ], [ 12, 78, 4 ] ];
for( n in motif ){
    note = leadNote( n[ 2 ], n[ 3 ] );
    audioAdd( timeline=lead, sound=note, at=n[ 1 ] * step );
}
bass = audioFilter(
    audioOsc( "saw", audioFreq( 38 ), 0.3 ), "lowpass", 900, 2.4 );
mix = audioMix( [ drums, bass, pads, arp, lead, harp ] );
mix = audioMaster( audio=mix, target="streaming" );  // -14 LUFS
audioSave( mix, "theme.mp3" );

    Every one of these was written, not recorded.

    Fifteen complete pieces rendered by the module from a few dozen lines each. Nothing plays until you press a button.

    Everything on the JVM

    One module. The whole audio pipeline.

    Synthesis and mixing

    Band-limited oscillators, drums, filters, a tempo grid, named tracks and kick pumping.

    Text to speech

    Open license voices that download on first use, with speed, pauses and captions timed to the words.

    Score a video

    Cut music to the exact length of a picture, add a voice-over and mux it with FFmpeg. audioMux()

    Loudness and mastering

    Measure the EBU R128 way and deliver to a target with a mastering chain, compressor, limiter and EQ.

    Streaming-14 LUFS
    Podcast-16 LUFS
    Broadcast-23 LUFS

    Game audio

    A real time mixer with buses, loops and crossfades, varied effects and audio sprites.

    Time and pitch

    Stretch, pitch shift and fit a clip to an exact length without the chipmunk.

    Analysis

    FFT, spectrum, tempo and silence detection, plus true peak.

    A real command line

    bx-audio info, master, speak and more. Downloads live in ~/.bxaudio.

    Turn the knobs. Hear the idea.
