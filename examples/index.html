    Examples

    Fifteen complete pieces made from code, each with a player.

    Every example is a BoxLang script that makes a complete piece of sound from code, with no samples and no licensing questions: all the music is original. The clips below are short renders at 96 kbps; the scripts make the full pieces. Each is one readable file in examples/, covered by tests that render it and measure what it sounds like.

    bx-audio pieces                         # the list
bx-audio render house --out=house.mp3   # render one, with its own settings
bx-audio explorer                       # play them all in the browser

    Pop Drive

    Upbeat pop. Voices, a beat grid, kick pump and mastering. A port of a numpy script, checked against the original.

    bx-audio render pop-drive --out=pop-drive.mp3
    Setting RangeDefault
    --barsBars8 to 5628
    --bpmTempo90 to 150128
    --styleStylepop, drivepop

    Script and notes

    Lofi

    Lofi hip hop. Swing, humanised timing, tape wobble and vinyl crackle over jazzy seventh chords.

    bx-audio render lofi --out=lofi.mp3
    Setting RangeDefault
    --barsBars8 to 3216
    --bpmTempo60 to 9578
    --swingSwing0.5 to 0.70.58
    --seedSeed1 to 997

    Script and notes

    House

    House. Four on the floor with sidechain pumping, a rolling bass, stabs, a riser and a drop.

    bx-audio render house --out=house.mp3
    Setting RangeDefault
    --barsBars16 to 4832
    --bpmTempo118 to 130124

    Drone and pads. Slow envelopes, inharmonic bells, noise swells and a very large reverb.

    bx-audio render ambient --out=ambient.mp3
    Setting RangeDefault
    --barsBars4 to 2412
    --bpmTempo36 to 7048
    --seedSeed1 to 995

    bx-audio render synthwave --out=synthwave.mp3
    Setting RangeDefault
    --barsBars16 to 4832

    Cinematic

    Trailer music. Tension, a build, one silent beat, then a huge boom, taiko drums and a theme. Ends on a ringing hit.

    bx-audio render cinematic --out=cinematic.mp3
    Setting RangeDefault
    --barsBars16 to 4024
    --bpmTempo70 to 12090

    Script and notes

    Chiptune

    8 bit game music. Original music for an imaginary console: two pulse channels, a triangle bass and noise drums. Five tracks, from an overworld theme to a boss fight.

    bx-audio render chiptune --out=chiptune.mp3
    Setting RangeDefault
    --trackTrackoverworld, cave, boss, title, victoryoverworld
    --loopsLoops (0 is the track's own)0 to 40

    Script and notes

    Game sound effects

    Sound effects. A jump, a coin, a laser, an explosion and more, made from pulse waves and noise. Hear them all in the sampler or one at a time.

    bx-audio render sfx --out=sfx.mp3
    Setting RangeDefault
    --soundSoundsampler, jump, coin, laser, explosion, powerup, hit, blip, alarm, gameover, footstepsampler
    --seedSeed (the noise in the explosion, hit and footstep)1 to 991

    Script and notes

    Jazz

    Swing blues. A trio on a twelve bar blues in F: walking bass, ride, piano comping and an improvised solo. Change the seed for another solo.

    bx-audio render jazz --out=jazz.mp3
    Setting RangeDefault
    --chorusesChoruses2 to 63
    --bpmTempo100 to 180132
    --swingSwing0.5 to 0.750.64
    --seedSeed1 to 994

    Script and notes

    bx-audio render rock --out=rock.mp3
    Setting RangeDefault

    --bpmTempo100 to 160128
    --seedSeed1 to 993

    Script and notes

    Voice-over

    Narration over music. A trailer style announcement: a text to speech voice over music that ducks under it. Needs the speech engine, so it runs from the command line.

    bx-audio render voiceover --out=voiceover.mp3
    Setting RangeDefault
    --voiceVoicetextkristin
    --textLines, separated by |textnone
    --duckMusic ducking in dB-24 to 0-12
    --bpmTempo80 to 130100

    Script and notes

    Trailer soundtrack

    A scored video. Music cut to an exact length, a voice-over with captions, ducking, mastering to a loudness target and chapter markers. Needs the speech engine, so it runs from the command line.

    bx-audio render trailer --out=trailer.mp3
    Setting RangeDefault
    --secondsLength in seconds8 to 4015
    --voiceVoicetextkristin
    --textWhat the voice saystextnone
    --targetLoudness targetstreaming, podcast, game, broadcast, cinemastreaming

    Script and notes

    Game audio

    A scripted play session. What a game asks of a sound engine: effects with variations, a looping theme, a boss theme made with time stretch and pitch shift, and a real time mixer with music and effects buses. A scripted session is rendered here.

    bx-audio render game-audio --out=game-audio.mp3
    Setting RangeDefault
    --seedSeed (changes the variations)1 to 201

    The BxAudio theme

    Grid anthem. A neon grid runner: a pulsing saw bass, gated pads, an opening filter, then an original adventure fanfare of rising fourths with a harp run.

    bx-audio render theme --out=theme.mp3
    Setting RangeDefault
    --barsBars8 to 3216
    --bpmTempo120 to 145132
