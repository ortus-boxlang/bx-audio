# Jazz

A trio playing a twelve bar blues in F at 132 BPM with a swing feel: a walking bass, a ride cymbal with a chick on two and four, an electric piano comping on the off beats, and a saxophone like lead. The first chorus plays the head, the next choruses are improvised solos, and the last chorus plays the head again and ends on a held F.

```bash
boxlang jazz.bxs                              # 3 choruses, about 69 seconds, out/jazz.wav
boxlang jazz.bxs --choruses=5 --seed=12       # a longer one, with other solos
boxlang jazz.bxs --bpm=160 --swing=0.58       # faster, with a lighter swing
```

| Setting | Default | |
|---|---|---|
| `--out` | `out/jazz.wav` | Where to write the audio |
| `--choruses` | 3 | Times through the twelve bars, at least 2 |
| `--bpm` | 132 | Tempo |
| `--swing` | 0.64 | Where the off beat eighth falls in the beat: 0.5 is straight, 0.667 is a triplet feel |
| `--seed` | 4 | The seed of the improvised solo, the walking bass notes and the drummer's ghost notes |

## Nothing is written note for note

The chord chart is data (F7, Bb7, F7, F7, Bb7, Bb7, F7, D7, Gm7, C7, F7 D7, Gm7 C7). Everything played over it is generated by rules, with a seeded random number generator so the same seed gives the same performance.

| Part | The rule |
|---|---|
| Bass | One note a beat. The root on beat one, then chord tones, then a half step above or below the next bar's root to lead into it |
| Piano | A rootless voicing (third, seventh, ninth and thirteenth, or the eleventh on a minor chord). A chord on one, one on the off beat of two, and sometimes one on the off beat of four |
| Drums | Ride on every beat plus the swung skip note after two and four, a chick on two and four, a feathered kick, now and then a ghost snare |
| Solo | Eighth notes through the scale of each chord (Mixolydian over a dominant, Dorian over a minor seventh). It lands on a chord tone on beats one and three, moves mostly by step, sometimes by a third, now and then turns around, and breathes for the last three eighths of every second bar |
| Head | The same generator with a sparse setting and a fixed seed, written once, so the last chorus plays exactly what the first did |

## What it shows

| Feature | Where |
|---|---|
| Swing as one number: the off beats are late | `eighthTime()`, `--swing` |
| A twelve bar blues as data, two chords to a bar | `blues` |
| Rule based improvisation that follows the chords | `soloBar()`, `scaleNotes()` |
| A seeded Park Miller random number generator | `rnd()`, `seedRandom()` |
| Voicings and a walking bass worked out from the chord symbol | `voicing()`, `bassMidi()` |

## Checked by measurement

Measured on two choruses, as the tests render them.

| What | Result |
|---|---|
| Harmony | on the first beat of each of the 24 bars the bass note is the root of the bar's chord, measured against the other four roots of the blues |
| Pulse of the low end at the beat | above 0.5, the walking bass |
| Highs (above 5 kHz) relative to the whole | more than 25 dB down, a warm mix |
| Length | the choruses at the tempo, plus about 3.8 seconds for the held chord and the reverb |
| Same seed | gives the identical file, another seed another solo, and the swing changes the feel but not the length |

The tests render two choruses and check these.
